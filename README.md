# Beauty & Wellness AI-Powered Customer Personalization Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze customer behavior, identify customer segments, understand shopping preferences, and support AI-driven personalization strategies in the Beauty & Wellness industry.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project explores customer demographics, product preferences, shopping channels, purchasing frequency, and budget preferences to help beauty and wellness brands develop more personalized marketing strategies.

The dashboard transforms customer data into actionable insights that support customer engagement, targeted campaigns, and data-driven business decisions.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Customer Segmentation:** Analyze the distribution of five behavioral customer segments.
- **Product Category Analysis:** Identify popular product categories and customer preferences.
- **Shopping Channel Analysis:** Compare customer engagement across marketplaces, apps, and other channels.
- **Purchase Frequency Analysis:** Understand recurring shopping patterns.
- **Budget Preference Analysis:** Explore Budget, Value, Premium, and Luxury preferences.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Filter insights by location, age group, product category, and customer segment.
- **Personalization Opportunities:** Identify opportunities for targeted promotions, product recommendations, and customer retention campaigns.

## 📈 Key Insights

- **51% Combined Segment Share:** GenZ Trend Explorers and Wellness Conscious Routiners together represent 51% of customers.
- **Skincare Preference:** Skincare is the leading preferred product category.
- **Marketplace Leadership:** Marketplaces such as Amazon and Myntra are the leading shopping channel.
- **Monthly Purchases:** Monthly purchasing is the most common reported buying frequency.
- **Diverse Budget Preferences:** Budget, Value, Premium, and Luxury preferences are distributed evenly across the customer base.

*These findings are based on the 100-consumer dataset used in this project.*

## 🤖 AI-Powered Personalization Strategy

The dashboard supports the development of data-driven personalization strategies:

1. **Personalized Product Recommendations:** Suggest products based on customer segments and preferred categories.
2. **Targeted Marketing Campaigns:** Tailor campaign messages to customer interests, age groups, and budget preferences.
3. **Customer Retention:** Explore replenishment reminders and subscription offers for recurring purchases.
4. **Channel Optimization:** Prioritize marketplaces and strengthen app and social-commerce experiences.
5. **Segment-Based Offers:** Design relevant promotions for trend-focused, wellness-conscious, and other customer groups.

*These are proposed personalization strategies supported by the analysis, not claims that a machine-learning recommendation engine has already been deployed.*

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize GenZ Trend Explorers and Wellness Conscious Routiners in relevant campaigns.
- Develop personalized skincare recommendations.
- Strengthen the marketplace and mobile-app shopping experience.
- Test social-commerce campaigns to improve customer engagement.
- Introduce replenishment reminders and subscription offers for suitable products.
- Measure campaign performance using conversion rate, repeat purchase rate, average order value, and customer engagement.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Install Power BI Desktop if it is not already installed.
3. Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
4. Explore the dashboard using the available slicers.
5. Compare customer segments, product preferences, purchasing patterns, and budget insights.
6. Use the findings to develop and evaluate personalization strategies.
